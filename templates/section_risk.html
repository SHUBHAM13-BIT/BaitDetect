Category Scoring Caps & Normalization

To prevent single vectors from distorting composite scores and to eliminate double-counting, each category is bounded by a ceiling cap:

Critical Override Rule & Heuristic Disclaimer

Critical Indicator Escalation:

If two or more Critical severity indicators are detected simultaneously (e.g. an executable double extension alongside a spoofed brand domain), the composite score is automatically elevated to at least 85/100 (Critical) regardless of other clean sections.

Scientific Heuristic Transparency:

BaitDetect uses transparent, rule-based static heuristics. While effective at intercepting common and sophisticated campaigns, heuristic scoring evaluates behavioral patterns and provides probabilistic risk indicators, not definitive mathematical proof of malicious intent.
