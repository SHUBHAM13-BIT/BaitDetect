Authentication Protocols Explained

SPF (Sender Policy Framework):

Specifies authorized sending IP addresses in domain DNS TXT records. Our analyzer checks headers and queries live DNS, clearly separating FAIL (unauthorized host) from MISSING (no policy declared).

DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail):

Verifies cryptographic public-key signatures ensuring message body and headers were not altered in transit.

DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication):

Combines SPF and DKIM with domain alignment policy (p=reject, p=quarantine, p=none), preventing display name and domain spoofing.

Envelope Mismatch Detection

From vs. Reply-To Mismatch:

Attackers display a legitimate brand in the From: header but configure Reply-To: to redirect responses to a burner address or free-mail account (Gmail, ProtonMail).

Display Name Spoofing:

The human-readable sender name claims to be "Microsoft Security" or "CEO" while the underlying SMTP address belongs to an arbitrary unauthenticated domain.

Received Hops Analysis:

Traces message transit across MTAs from origin to destination, isolating the true sending IP and flagging anomalous routing patterns.
